01

Placement

My national service placement is with Tullow Oil's Well Delivery Team in Takoradi — one of Ghana's most active upstream operators in the Jubilee and TEN fields.

The Well Delivery Team is responsible for the planning, execution, and integrity of well operations — from drilling through completions and handover. Being embedded here means working with real operational data, real equipment, and real constraints on active subsea projects.

Day-to-day work spans reviewing technical documentation, supporting equipment tracking, engaging with vendor representatives, and building familiarity with the full well delivery lifecycle from a subsea and completions perspective.

Due to the nature of upstream operations, project-specific data and internal deliverables remain confidential. What I can share is the knowledge base, the equipment exposure, and the context that comes from working inside the operation.

04

The Differentiator

Most petroleum engineers don't think about cybersecurity. Most cybersecurity professionals have never been inside an oil and gas operation. That gap is where the risk lives — and it's exactly where I sit.

Subsea infrastructure, drilling control systems, and intelligent completion tools all run on industrial control networks. These networks are increasingly connected, increasingly targeted, and almost universally secured by people who have never seen the physical systems they're protecting.

My background gives me something most ICS/OT security practitioners don't have: context from the inside. I know what a Christmas tree system does, how downhole data flows, and what a production anomaly looks like. That's not something you learn from a certification.
