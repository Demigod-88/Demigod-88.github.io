01 — About

From Field Operations to Data Engineering

Petrochemical engineer with hands-on upstream oil & gas experience, now building data systems that solve real operational problems.

I'm a BSc. Petrochemical Engineering graduate from KNUST with direct experience in well engineering, fuel supply operations, and hydraulic systems maintenance. My transition into data engineering wasn't a pivot away from oil & gas — it was a response to the operational pain I saw firsthand.

At Tullow Ghana, I didn't just manage chemical inventory — I automated the entire tracking system for 100+ drilling products, built cross-departmental BI dashboards, and shipped automation tools for Finance, IT, Facilities, and Marine teams. I understand the data because I understand the operations behind it.

Certified Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer Associate and Google Advanced Data Analytics Professional. I combine SQL, Power BI, Power Automate, and Python with domain knowledge that most data engineers don't have: I know what a Christmas tree system does, how downhole data flows, and why a BOSIET expiry alert actually matters.
