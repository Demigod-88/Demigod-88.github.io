01 — About

Engineer first. Data professional second.

I didn't move into data from a bootcamp. I moved into it from the field — because I saw how much operational pain came from bad data processes.

I'm a BSc. Petrochemical Engineering graduate from KNUST with direct upstream oil & gas experience — well engineering, fuel supply operations, and hydraulic systems. That background means I don't just build dashboards; I understand the operations, compliance requirements, and stakeholder pressures behind the data.

At Tullow Ghana, I built and shipped automation and BI tools across four departments — not as side projects, but as production systems used by real teams. Chemical inventory tracking, invoice automation, IT asset management, accommodation utilization, safety compliance alerts, and marine fleet dashboards.

Certified Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer Associate and Google Advanced Data Analytics Professional. I work across the full data lifecycle: ingestion, transformation, modeling, and visualization — with a bias toward making systems that outlast my involvement.
