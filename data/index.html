02

Projects

JD_001
Mock Exam Tracker
Excel Tracking Education
View on GitHub →

Students write a high volume of mock examinations throughout the academic year. Manually tracking scores across multiple sittings, subjects, and students becomes unmanageable fast — results get lost, comparisons become impossible, and trends go unnoticed.

Consolidates scores across multiple mock sittings into a single structured view per student
Tracks performance across subjects and over time to surface trends early
Eliminates the manual effort of cross-referencing paper records or scattered spreadsheets
JD_002
SBA Tracker & Report Card Generator
Excel Automation Education
View on GitHub →

School-Based Assessments accumulate across every subject, every term, every year — for every student. The end-of-term report card that should take minutes ends up taking days when the data is scattered and the process is manual.

Aggregates all SBA results across subjects and terms into a persistent student record
Tracks each student's academic trajectory from enrollment forward, year over year
Automatically generates end-of-term report cards — no manual compilation required
